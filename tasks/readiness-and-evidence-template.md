# Readiness and evidence: reusable exercise template

Draft design guide · September 22, 2026. Use during the teaching-transfer stage of the [interactive failure-mode lab](../labs/failure-mode-lab/README.md). The assistant gathers the educator's decisions conversationally, one question at a time; this is not a form to finish before starting. An unresolved field stays unresolved; the assistant must not invent it.
