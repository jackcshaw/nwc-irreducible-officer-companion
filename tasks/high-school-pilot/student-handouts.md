Name or class code: ____________________    Date: ____________________

Work individually with your usual access supports. Put away the earlier pages. Use only this page; do not use AI or discuss answers. These data and the investigation are fictional.

A team uses two same-size, dry asphalt tiles from the same batch, side by side. Both start at 30 °C. A coin toss selects one tile for a screen that blocks direct sunlight; the other remains in full sun. Both are measured after the same 30-minute interval, using checked sensors and the same method. | --- | --- | --- |
